How I work

From a whiteboard conversation to a system people actually use.

Most AI projects stall between the demo and the workflow. My job is closing that gap, end to end.

01

Discover

Sit with the team, map the real process, and find the steps that cost the most time or break most often.

02

Architect

Pick the simplest design that works: which agent, which tools, where humans approve, and how it fails safely.

03

Build & integrate

Ship it into the tools people already use (CRMs, chat, calendars, cloud) instead of yet another dashboard.

04

Operate

Deploy, monitor and iterate. Self-hosted where it makes sense, with costs and limits people can see.

Helix: goal-driven coordination for Claude agents

Creator · open source (MIT)
Problem
Single agents are good at tasks but bad at long-running goals: they lose context, use the wrong tools and nobody checks their work.
What I built
A desktop engine that turns a goal into a kanban board of tasks. A planner agent breaks the goal down, each task runs as a Claude subprocess with only the MCP tools scoped to that goal, and every result is verified before the next task is chained on.
Outcome
A reusable pattern for putting agents to work safely: scoped tools, human approval and verification by default.

Accountify: automated accounting & tax

Architect & builder
Problem
Running a small company in Portugal means double-entry books, VAT, corporate tax and certified invoicing: a lot of rules, and costly mistakes.
What I built
A modular monolith with ports & adapters: a real double-entry ledger (Blnk) with a single writer that enforces balance, idempotency and period locks, and a compliance layer for SNC, IVA/IRC, SAF-T and certified invoices. Decimal money and typed, validated boundaries throughout.
Outcome
Bookkeeping and filings that are correct by construction, not by careful data entry.
